# Expand the Dharma Jewellery inner pages

## Goal
Bring the uploaded Dharma Jewellery shop into the current project, preserve its luxury visual direction, and turn the sparse inner pages into complete, mobile-friendly experiences.

## Pages and sections
- **Collections:** retain filtering and products, then add shopping assurances and a personal styling call-to-action.
- **Product:** retain the gallery and purchase controls, then strengthen product details, care/delivery information, and related-piece discovery.
- **Our Story:** add the brand philosophy, heritage/craftsmanship story, values, and a collection call-to-action.
- **Bespoke:** add a clear consultation-to-completion process, service highlights, and appointment call-to-action.
- **Experience:** add consultation options, appointment journey, service promises, and booking call-to-action.
- **Journal:** add responsive editorial story cards, topic highlights, and a collection call-to-action.
- **Stores:** add visit planning, in-store services, and contact/appointment actions without inventing addresses.
- Keep cart, wishlist, checkout, and orders focused as utility pages, while checking their mobile layout.

## Mobile behavior
- Use single-column reading flow and stack actions on small screens.
- Use stable responsive grids for feature, process, editorial, and product sections.
- Prevent header, product controls, headings, and cards from clipping or overflowing.
- Keep touch targets comfortably sized and preserve concise content hierarchy.

## Technical details
- Copy only the uploaded app source/configuration and exclude its Git metadata and generated dependencies.
- Reuse its existing assets, shared shell, semantic color tokens, and route structure.
- Add shared section patterns where practical so visual rhythm remains consistent.
- Complete route-specific social metadata fields, then validate key pages at desktop and mobile widths.
